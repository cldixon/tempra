Connect an agent

  1. 1
    In Claude, open Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector and paste this URL:
    https://app.tempra.dev/mcp
  2. 2 Approve the connection in the window that opens: all libraries, or the ones you pick, and whether it may create new ones.
  3. 3
    Ask it to start, for example "Open my tempra-dev library and summarise what changed this week."
    Waiting for your agent's first call…
Or make a token instead.
